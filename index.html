Yushan Zhang

I'm a WASP funded PhD Student at Linköping Universion in Sweden, supervised by Prof. Michael Felsberg. I was a research intern at NVIDIA Dynamic Vision and Learning Group lead by Laura Leal-Taixe.

I'm interested in 3D Computer Vision in general, including 3D point cloud perception, Scene flow estimation, Computer vision for robotics, 3D scene understanding, Open-vocabulary object segmentation and tracking, Diffusion models, Vision-language models etc.
